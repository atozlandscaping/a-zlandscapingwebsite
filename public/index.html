Our specialty

Wildfire mitigation that looks good, too.

In the high country, your landscape can either feed a fire or slow it down. We've completed significant hardscape projects built around reducing wildfire risk — swapping out fuel-heavy plantings and mulch for rock, stone and water-wise design that holds up to mountain weather and looks great doing it.

Talk to us about your property Free resource Summit County Defensible Space Guide How to protect your property, from the county's official site ↗
  1. 01

    Clear the area next to your home

    Replace bark mulch and dense plantings near the house with landscape rock, gravel and flagstone.

  2. 02

    Thin and limb trees

    Remove dead or crowded trees and limb up branches to break the path a fire can climb.

  3. 03

    Go xeroscape

    Low-water, fire-wise plantings and rock gardens that cut maintenance and watering.

  4. 04

    Haul it all away

    Slash, logs, needles and debris cleaned up and removed so nothing is left behind.

Our story

Two brothers. One crew. A to Z.

A-Z Landscaping is owned and operated by brothers Aidan and Brendan Collins. We started the company in May 2023 with a simple idea: be the crew mountain homeowners can call for just about anything outside — and plenty inside, too.

When you call A-Z, you're talking to the owners, and the owners are on the job. We show up when we say we will, leave every property cleaner than we found it, and take the same pride in a weekly mow as we do in a flagstone patio.

— Aidan & Brendan Collins, Owners
